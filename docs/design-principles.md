# Design Principles

The app should feel like a calm TFT coach, not a debug panel.

## In Game

- Keep the overlay compact and readable at a glance.
- Show only what matters now: comp direction, shop signal, economy action, augments, and the early/mid/late plan.
- Prefer short labels and stable sections over long paragraphs.
- Use a restrained dark neutral surface with gold and teal accents for hierarchy.
- Avoid blocking the center board, shop, or augment cards.
- Make the overlay draggable and easy to hide.

## Desktop App

- The desktop window is the control center: data updates, calibration, history, and editing comps.
- It should stay quiet, organized, and utilitarian.
- Debug details belong in a separate area, never in the primary in-game overlay.
